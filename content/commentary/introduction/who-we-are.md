We are three laymen. Though each of us attended Christian universities, none of us studied the Bible or theology. As a result, the sum total of our formal education amounts to roughly the equivalent of that received by a first-year divinity student. We are fathers, realtors, engineers, and lawn care professionals.

Yet each of our lives has been changed by a story. This story, while personal and life-changing as each of us can attest, is cosmic in scope. It is the story of a God who has no rivals, yet whose creation, both gods and men, joins in rebellion against Him. This God, Yahweh, began a project, a creative work, eons ago, and He is perfecting that same work in our lives today.

Genesis is the beginning of this story. It is Yahweh's story, and yet we find ourselves within it. We are both unqualified to approach this story and compelled to do so with fear and trembling. Yet we also feel a jealous ownership of this story as our own, and we will not hesitate to go to blows with anyone whom we believe misrepresents its message.

It is unsurprising, then, that this work is full of unabashed evangelical fervor. Yet we hope you will find it every bit as full of thoughtful, well-researched scholarly rigor. For the latter, we are indebted to the life work of the learned pastors and scholars whose writings we have read and from whom we have been mentored. For the former, we are indebted to the work of the Holy Spirit, who has breathed life into our dead hearts and minds so that we may see, with new-creation eyes, the goodness of the God this book proclaims.
