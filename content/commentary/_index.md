# Welcome!
---
This site is the result of a class that was taught at our church on the primeval history found in the first eleven chapters of Genesis. This study has so profoundly impacted our lives and shaped our understanding of Scripture that we wanted to preserve it in a permanent form for future reference and for distribution to the body of Christ beyond our small class.

Our hope is that all who read these pages will deepen their understanding of these life-giving texts. We also pray that, through a clearer understanding of the opening pages of God's grand narrative, readers will better grasp the gospel story that runs throughout the whole Bible: the story of Christ enthroned in His creation temple, reigning over those in heaven, those on earth, and those under the earth, forever and ever. Amen!
